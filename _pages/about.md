I am a PhD candidate in Explainable AI (XAI) under the supervision of Prof. [Christos
Diou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christos-diou/?originalSubdomain=gr),
Prof. [Eirini
Ntoutsi](https://www.mi.fu-berlin.de/en/inf/groups/ag-KIML/members/Professoren/Ntoutsi.html)
and [Theodore
Dalamagas](https://scholar.google.gr/citations?user=WJOLNAYAAAAJ&hl=en). 

---------------

◍ I do research on (a) explainable-by-design models and
(b) explainability techniques for black-box models when the use of
the latter is the only effective solution. In all cases, I try to
model the uncertainty.
