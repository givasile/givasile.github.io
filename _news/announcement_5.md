From March of 2022, I am a PhD candidate in AI under the supervision
of Prof. [Christos
Diou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christos-diou/?originalSubdomain=gr),
Prof. [Eirini
Ntoutsi](https://www.unibw.de/home-en/appointment-of-professors/prof-eirini-ntoutsi)
and Prof. [Theodore
Dalamagas](https://scholar.google.gr/citations?user=WJOLNAYAAAAJ&hl=en).
The PhD is a cooperation between the Harokopio University of Athens
(primary affiliation) and the ATHENA Research Center.

My PhD research focuses on Explainable AI, specifically in extending
traditional Explainability techniques to be applicable in
probabilistic ML models, e.g. Bayesian Neural Networks.
