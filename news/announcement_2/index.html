Started working as Research Assistant at the Mulitmedia Understanding Group (MUG)

Created in February 01, 2018

2018

I worked as a Research Assistant at the Multimedia Understanding Group (MUG) from February of 2018 until September of 2020. I was mainly involved in the Horizon-2020 research project BigO, where my primary responsibility was extracting users’ daily activity and habits (sleeping, sitting, walking etc.) from accelerometer and gyroscope signals using machine learning and signal processing techniques.
